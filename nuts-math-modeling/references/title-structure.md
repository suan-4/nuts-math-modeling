# Title Structure and Process Placement

## Chapter rule

For a multi-question paper, use **一问一个一级标题** whenever each question adds a material mechanism, dimension, or solver change. A typical optimization paper is:

```text
Question 1: base formulation
Question 2: dimensional/model extension and new solver need
Question 3: new mechanism and resulting decision change
Model evaluation and extension
```

Do not force an independent "model checking" chapter when validation is evidence for a specific question. **验证应融入**产生该结果的问题正文：put feasibility checks, convergence evidence, and sensitivity next to the question whose result they support. Cross-question checks may follow the final question under a concrete cross-question title.

## Specific-title test

A title must answer one of: what is formulated, what constraint/mechanism is derived, what solver action is performed, what evidence is checked, or what decision/result is explained.

Replace generic titles such as "约束条件", "模型求解与过程", and "结果分析" with titles such as:

- "跨季轮作链与特殊作物窗口约束";
- "按土地类型分解的 Fix-and-Optimize 滚动求解";
- "销售结构、超产成因与需求敏感性分析".

Keep heading depth at three levels or fewer. Use numbered steps inside a subsection rather than adding a fourth heading level.

Keep titles short enough to navigate. This project's approved checking headings use “问题一模型的检验” and equivalent question-specific forms. Do not lengthen every heading mechanically or insert a visible table of contents when the selected template forbids it.

At a major modelling chapter add a brief concrete lead-in. Under intermediate headings add text only if needed to connect their subsections; headings alone are not a reason for filler. See [process-writing.md](process-writing.md).

## Background and closing chapters

Write the background as `policy/industry context -> current contradiction -> why this decision matters -> question mapping`. Cite factual policy claims when needed. Do not use generic agricultural, digital, or sustainability prose solely to increase length.

Use the final evaluation chapter to synthesize evidence, scope boundaries, and extensions. Do not repeat every result. Aim for a proportionate closing section, usually about one normal manuscript page when the paper is otherwise full length.

## Word navigation

Apply real Word heading styles or an equivalent verified outline level to chapter, section, and subsection paragraphs. Visible bold text alone does not create a navigable outline or reliable table of contents.
